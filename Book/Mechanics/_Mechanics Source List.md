## Approved YouTube Channels

These are channels we draw from regularly. When adding video resources to any page, check here first.

### The Efficient Engineer
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@TheEfficientEngineer](https://www.youtube.com/@TheEfficientEngineer)
- **Why:** The single most-used channel in the vault. Polished animations, "Understanding X" series covers nearly every mechanics of materials topic we have — stress, strain, Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, torsion, failure theories, area moment of inertia, beam deflection, and more. First stop for any new mechanics page.

### The Engineering Mindset
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@EngineeringMindset](https://www.youtube.com/@EngineeringMindset)
- **Why:** Clear, visual explanations of mechanical and electrical engineering fundamentals. Strong on beam diagrams and stresses in beams. Good pacing for students encountering these ideas for the first time.

### Brian B Douglas
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@BrianBDouglas](https://www.youtube.com/@BrianBDouglas)
- **Why:** Best-in-class control systems content. Intuitive explanations of transfer functions, Bode plots, PID, root locus, and state space. Ideal companion for our controls pages.

### MATLAB — Tech Talks Series
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@MATLAB](https://www.youtube.com/@MATLAB)
- **Why:** The [Tech Talks playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLn8PRpmsu08qL-MT3JJRN7v7sFHZkBGAv) covers control theory, signal processing, and modeling concepts with excellent animations. Prefer Tech Talks videos over general MATLAB tutorials for conceptual pages.

### The Organic Chemistry Tutor
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@TheOrganicChemistryTutor](https://www.youtube.com/@TheOrganicChemistryTutor)
- **Why:** Despite the name, covers physics and statics topics thoroughly. Good for foundational mechanics content like free body diagrams and Newton's laws where a first-principles physics approach is helpful.

### Engineer4Free
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@Engineer4Free](https://www.youtube.com/@Engineer4Free)
- **Why:** 600+ free engineering tutorials covering statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, circuits, fluid mechanics, and more. Example-driven and practical. Confirmed in use for Hooke's Law, Normal Stress, and Beam Deflection (superposition method).

### Brian Bernard Engineering
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@BrianBernardEngineering](https://www.youtube.com/@BrianBernardEngineering)
- **Why:** Associate Professor at Schreiner University. Short, information-dense videos aimed at helping engineering students pass their classes. Good supplemental source for mechanics of materials and related topics.

### Jeff Hanson
- **Channel:** [youtube.com/@1234jhanson](https://www.youtube.com/@1234jhanson)
- **Why:** Texas Tech professor with a complete Statics and Mechanics of Materials video series. Practical, example-driven style. Good for statics theory topics — support reactions, internal forces, equilibrium. Also authored *How to Ace Statics*.

## Using Outside Sources

If a topic isn't covered by the channels above, outside sources are fine — but prefer channels with:
- Consistent visual style and pacing
- No excessive ads or sponsorship interruptions mid-explanation
- Conceptual depth over step-by-step calculation demos

When a new channel is used more than once, add it to the Approved Channels section above.
